# CreatorForge — what it does for you

Hey! This is the app being built for you. Plain English, no tech degree
needed. Here's everything it does, organized by what you actually want:
make content, talk to fans, and get paid — with less of the draining parts.

---

## 1. Organize all your content in one place

Point it at a folder of your photos and videos and it builds you a
searchable library: it finds duplicates automatically, tags everything,
and lets you search it later ("show me all the red-lingerie pics").
Anything the AI makes for you gets filed back in automatically too.

## 2. Make AI videos of yourself — so you can film less

This is the big one. The app can generate realistic videos of *you*
based on *your own* existing content. How it works, honestly:

- You pick which of your photos/videos to train from.
- That trains your **personal AI model** (more on that word below) — a
  digital version of your look that only you control.
- Then you describe a **scene** — the setting, outfit, camera angle, vibe
  — and it generates the video.

**Your comfort rules are built in.** You set which categories you'll film
yourself and which are **AI-only** (stuff you'd rather not actually do on
camera). The app refuses to cross that line, every time, and logs every
decision so you can check.

**Nothing generates without your signed consent.** Before anything runs,
you sign a consent form (a template is included) saying what your likeness
may be used for. No signed consent = the app refuses. Period. That lock is
in the code itself, not just a promise.

## 3. Chat with fans without living in your DMs

The app drafts replies for you — in **your** texting voice. It can learn
how you actually text (your slang, your rhythm, your emojis) from your
exported chats, and it shows you a report of what it learned so you can
tweak it.

You stay in charge three ways:
- **Approve-first (default):** every draft waits for your tap before
  anything goes out. Nothing ever sends itself.
- **Manual:** the bot stays quiet and just logs messages for you to
  answer yourself.
- **Auto:** only if you explicitly turn it on, with a big warning.

It also flags messages that need your eyes — angry fans, refund talk,
anything risky — and it can answer "what's your menu?" style questions
automatically from your tip menu.

## 4. Spicy mode (the stuff you asked for)

A separate chat mode for flirty/dirty talk, with levels you control:
playful → teasing → explicit. **You** set the level, and the bot never
raises it on its own. The explicit level starts empty — you write every
word of it yourself.

Money-making flows built in:
- **Paid "rates":** a fan sends a pic asking for a rating → the app
  drafts a funny scorecard reply plus a tip upsell with your payment link.
- **Tip requests:** "how much for a custom?" → it replies with the item,
  *your* price from *your* menu (it never invents prices), and your
  Cash App link.
- **Pic triage:** fan-sent photos land in a queue as **blurred
  thumbnails**. You tap approve or skip — no more staring at a hundred
  unsolicited pics to find the paying customers.

## 5. Sell custom videos on autopilot (mostly)

Fans pick a scene from **your** menu, pay through **your** links, and the
order goes into a queue: paid → AI generates it → **you watch the
finished video and approve it** → then it's delivered. If a fan tries to
order something in one of your AI-only or refused categories, the order
is rejected outright. You always see the final product before it goes out.

## 6. Go live without being there

For video calls and livestreams, your AI model can appear instead of you —
either through a real avatar service or a free do-it-yourself setup using
**OBS** (free streaming software) as a virtual camera. There's an honest
guide covering what each option costs, how much delay to expect, and which
platforms allow it vs. which ones could ban the account (most cam sites
require a verified real person on camera — the guide says exactly where
the lines are).

**Spicy livestream platforms:** the app now connects to the big cam sites
(Chaturbate, Stripchat, BongaCams, CamSoda, ManyVids, and more). You can
run an **AFK stream** — your AI avatar goes live through OBS while you're
away, with chat auto-moderation and tip-triggered actions. Because most
sites require verified real broadcasters, the app makes you confirm you
understand the risk before going live, and the setup guide walks through
each site's rules honestly.

## 7. Get paid, your way

One command shows all your payment links — Venmo, Cash App, PayPal,
crypto, Throne, whatever you configure. It can also generate a
shareable **tip-menu page**: your menu items, prices, and pay links on one
nice page you can send fans.

## 8. Post everywhere, from one place

- **Reddit:** actually posts for you through Reddit's official system
  (always shows you a dry run first).
- **Snapchat, TikTok, OnlyFans, Fansly:** these sites don't *allow*
  auto-posting (bots get accounts permanently banned), so the app builds
  you a ready-to-post packet — media, caption, hashtags, checklist — and
  reminds you when it's due. You tap post in the app yourself.
- **Scheduler:** plan your week of posts; it reminds you what's due.

## 9. Know your numbers

Log what each post earns and what each platform pays you per month, and
get a plain-English report: totals, best platforms, top posts, and where
every number came from. Reddit stats pull in live automatically; the
others you enter (their sites don't share that data with outside apps).

**Fan book:** it keeps a smart address book of your fans — who's a big
tipper, who's gone quiet, who's new — so you always know who to message.
It can also draft welcome messages for new subs and win-back notes for
expired ones (you approve everything before it goes out).

## 10. Everything locked up

Your content and settings live in an encrypted **vault** — real
military-grade encryption, and the password is never stored anywhere.
Lose the password = nobody, not even us, can open it. You can back the
whole thing up as one encrypted file and restore it on a new computer.

## 11. Use it however you like

- **On your computer:** a desktop app with a frosted-glass look, or a
  simple menu in the terminal, or a web dashboard in your browser.
- **On your phone:** an Android app that talks to the computer app —
  approve chat drafts, check your triage queue, see orders, all from bed.

## Glossary — words you might not know

- **AI model (your "persona model"):** software that has learned what you
  look like from your photos, so it can generate new images/video of you.
  Think of it like a digital mannequin of you that only you own.
- **LoRA:** the specific technique used to teach an AI model your face.
  It's a small add-on file trained on ~20–200 of your photos. Training
  needs a powerful computer (a **GPU**) for a few hours, or you can pay
  an online service to do it.
- **GPU:** a powerful graphics chip (for gaming or AI). AI video needs
  one; a regular laptop can run everything else in this app fine.
- **Prompt:** the written description you give the AI ("you on a beach
  at sunset, red dress, slow turn to camera"). Better description =
  better video. The app saves your best ones to reuse.
- **Scene:** a saved setup — setting + outfit + camera + vibe — you can
  reuse or sell to fans as a custom video option.
- **PPV (pay-per-view):** content a fan unlocks by paying, like a locked
  photo or video in a DM.
- **Tip menu:** your price list — what costs what (pics, videos, calls,
  ratings...). Fans pick, pay, you deliver.
- **Rates:** fans pay you to rate their pics. Yes, really. It's a whole
  economy.
- **Findom:** "financial domination" — fans pay to be teased/denied by
  you. Strictly opt-in here, and you write every word.
- **Vault:** your encrypted storage locker inside the app. See #10.
- **Encryption:** scrambling your files with a password so they're
  unreadable to anyone without it — including us.
- **API:** a official doorway a website opens for apps to talk to it.
  Reddit has one (so auto-posting works); Snapchat/OnlyFans/Fansly don't
  (so the app helps you post manually instead of risking your account).
- **OBS:** free software streamers use to control what their camera
  shows — used here to put your AI model on live video instead of you.
- **RTMP:** the technical feed format livestream sites accept video in.
  You don't need to understand it — the app handles it.
- **PWA:** a website that can "install" on your phone like an app, no
  app store needed.
- **APK / EAS:** the Android app file format, and the service that
  builds it. You'll get a download link when it's built.
- **CRM:** "customer relationship management" — here, just a smart
  address book of your fans: who's a big tipper, who to win back, notes
  on everyone.
- **NSFW:** "not safe for work" — adult content. The triage queue blurs
  it until you choose to look.
- **Consent pack:** your signed permission slip (who you are, what you
  agreed to, when). The app checks it before every AI generation.
- **Dry run:** a practice run that shows what *would* happen without
  doing it — e.g. preview a Reddit post before it goes live.
